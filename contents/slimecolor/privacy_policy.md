# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

**Developer:** Elif Games

**App Name:** Slime Shows Its True Colors

## 1. Introduction

Elif Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Slime Shows Its True Colors ("the App").

The App does not require account registration or submission of personal information to play.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores limited gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

- Game scores and personal best scores.
- Local leaderboard entries, including the display name you enter when setting a new record.

This information is used solely to provide the game's features and is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers or associated with an online account.

## 3. Information Collection and Sharing

Elif Games does not collect, receive, sell, or share your locally stored gameplay information.

The App does not transmit your scores or leaderboard entries to external servers.

[This section assumes the App does not integrate advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other third-party SDKs that collect or transmit personal or device data. Update this policy if such services are integrated.]

## 4. External Links

The App may provide links to the Elif Games website and other games.

If you choose to open these links, you may be redirected to external websites or services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective privacy policies. Elif Games is not responsible for the privacy practices of external services.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

We do not maintain server-side copies of your gameplay scores or leaderboard entries.

## 6. Data Security

Gameplay information is stored within the App's local storage on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

We do not maintain an online database of player information.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page, with the effective date revised accordingly.
